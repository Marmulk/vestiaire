Vestiaire Support

A question, a problem, an idea? Write to vestiaire.assistance@icloud.com, mentioning your iPhone model and its iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

How should I photograph a piece?

Hang it on a hanger against a plain wall, front-on and well lit. The photo guide, when you add a piece, shows you how. Shoes and accessories are photographed resting on a plain background.

The analysis doesn’t fill in the details

Check your connection. The number of analyses is limited per day and per device: the quota resets the next day. You can always fill in the details yourself.

My pieces don’t appear on my other device

Check that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account, that iCloud is turned on for Vestiaire and that there is space left in your iCloud. The sync status is shown in the app’s Settings, under ‘iCloud sync’.

How do I back up my wardrobe?

In the app’s Settings, under ‘Backup’, tap ‘Export wardrobe’ and keep the file wherever you like. To restore it, open it with Vestiaire or tap ‘Restore a backup’. Anything missing is added, and nothing is erased. Vestiaire also keeps an automatic backup on the phone, renewed every day.

The weather doesn’t match my city

Allow location access, or choose a city manually with ‘City weather’.

How do I delete my data?

Delete pieces in the app, or delete the app: its local data disappears with it. For the iCloud copy, open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then iCloud: Vestiaire is listed in storage management.
